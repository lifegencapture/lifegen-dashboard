    Making your review copy

    Videos over 200 MB are shrunk into a review copy on your own computer (nothing is sent anywhere while it works), then uploaded here. It plays your video through once, so keep this tab open and on screen. Use Google Chrome.

    Tip: export a review copy straight from your editing app

    If this keeps failing, export a review copy from your editing app and upload that instead (it goes straight up, no shrinking): H.264 MP4, 1920×1080, bitrate 5 Mbps (1-pass VBR), audio AAC 128 kbps. That fits about 5 minutes in under 200 MB. For longer videos use 3 Mbps. The full-quality export still goes to Google Drive.
